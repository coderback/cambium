## Session 11: DGraph snapshot landed and verified — ADR-010 accepted, three discrepancies reconciled

- **Snapshot obtained and verified before any DGF-1 code exists** — which is exactly why the verifier was written first (ELL-1 precedent). `DGraphFin.zip` (150,476,320 B) → `data/dgraph/` (gitignored). It **escalated on arrival** rather than absorbing a mismatch, as designed. **ADR-010 accepted**; ~14 sites in doc-02 + timeline amended, then the constant changed — that ordering is the whole point.
- **Confirmed exactly:** 3,700,550 nodes · 4,300,999 edges · 17 features · timestamps 1–821 (821 distinct) · 15,509 fraud / 1,210,092 normal / 2,474,949 background. **Prevalence 1.2654%** — the figure **ADR-007 assumed** when fixing ROC-AUC + AUPRC, now measured rather than assumed.
- **THE FINDING: the official DGraphFin split is a RANDOM mask, not temporal.** Node-time proxy medians train/val/test = **216 / 217 / 215** — a spread of **2 steps out of 821**. doc-02 §2.3 made this an explicit "do not assume" verification task; it is now answered. **The published leaderboard numbers are therefore not a temporal holdout**, so they cannot carry DGF-1's structural claim (doc-00 §7). Gates move to **our own temporal split**; official-split numbers are reported-not-gated and always labelled.
- **This independently re-derives ADR-007's structure.** ADR-007 demoted the leaderboard clause because gating it means gating on a third party's run under an *unverified* protocol. It turns out the protocol is not merely unverified but **different in kind**. Two independent arguments now land on the same design.
- **`edge_type` is 1–11 (11 types), not 0–11 (12).** Contiguous, no type 0. PyG's own docstring says "0 to 11" and is wrong too — recorded in doc-02 §9 so nobody later "fixes" our number back to match it. Concrete consequence: the per-node edge-type histogram is **11 wide**; a 12th column would ship a permanently-zero feature.
- **The degree figure was a definitional trap.** "~1.16" is `E/N`, mean *out*-degree — but §2.2.4 makes reverse edges the **default**, so the sampled graph has mean total degree **2.325 ≈ Elliptic's 2.30**, median 2, **zero** isolated nodes, and **38.9%** (not "most") of nodes at ≤1 neighbour. Kept §0's small-delta expectation but **corrected its basis** to GADBench + the 38.9%; whether the delta really is small is Gate 1's question, not the doc's.
- **ADR-009 confirmed on measurement.** Its premise (fan-out cannot bind here) was argued from the *assumed* 1.16. Measured: only **0.26%** of nodes reach the smallest fan-out of 10 — so the conclusion holds under the *larger* degree convention. Batch size was right for stronger reasons than I had.
- **Two things I got wrong and caught:** my verifier printed `2E/N` next to the doc's `E/N` figure, reading as a contradiction when it was a definitional difference (now prints both, labelled); and applying the §2.3 amendment I duplicated the "Encode in `gbe.eval`" line — caught by grepping after, not by reading.
- **Also recorded:** background is **two** label values (`y∈{2,3}`), so `labelled_mask = (y==0)|(y==1)` — doc-02's "binary fraud label" is true of the task and false of the tensor.
- **Surprised:** the single most consequential fact about this dataset — that its benchmark split is random — was invisible from every document, the PyG docstring, and the dataset paper's framing. Only loading it and looking answered it, and it silently changes what DGF-1 can claim.
- **New work this created:** DGF-1 Phase 0 must **build and leakage-test its own temporal split**, and `gbe.eval.temporal` splits on *node* time while DGraph dates *edges*. That is an **EXTRACT design input** — decide it while cutting the `DataSource` seam, not after. Constraint fixed by ADR-010: a node's assigned time may not depend on any post-cutoff edge.
- **Next (researcher):** commit. Then EXTRACT — with the split-derivation question now on the table as part of the seam design. **DGraphFin-2 remains unassessed** (extension → defer per the Elliptic++ precedent; replacement → doc-02's pinned shape moves and needs its own ADR before Phase 0).
